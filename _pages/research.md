## Research Interests

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. I am particularly interested in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning** - Exploring how large language models can learn to reason through reinforcement learning techniques, including the synthesis of verifiable reasoning data at scale.

- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models** - Investigating the perception bottleneck of vision-language models for chart understanding and developing approaches to mitigate hallucinations in multimodal models.

- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability** - Studying the internal representations of LLMs to understand and improve their truthfulness, including the discovery of universal truthfulness hyperplanes and inner representation perspectives for hallucination mitigation.

## Research Projects

### SynLogic: Synthesizing Verifiable Reasoning Data at Scale for Learning Logical Reasoning and Beyond

**2025** | First Author

Synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale to enable learning logical reasoning and beyond. This work explores how to create high-quality reasoning datasets that can be used to train models with strong logical reasoning capabilities.

### On the Perception Bottleneck of VLMs for Chart Understanding

**2025** | First Author

Investigating the perception bottleneck of vision-language models when it comes to chart understanding. This work identifies the limitations of current VLMs in processing visual information from charts and proposes solutions to address these bottlenecks.

### On the Universal Truthfulness Hyperplane Inside LLMs

**2024** | First Author

Discovering the universal truthfulness hyperplane inside LLMs. This work explores the internal representations of large language models and identifies a hyperplane that separates truthful from non-truthful responses, enabling better understanding and control of model behavior.

### In-Context Sharpness as Alerts: An Inner Representation Perspective for Hallucination Mitigation

**2024** | Co-Author

Using in-context sharpness as alerts from an inner representation perspective for hallucination mitigation. This work proposes a novel approach to detect and mitigate hallucinations in LLMs by monitoring the sharpness of in-context representations.

### C-Eval: A Multi-Level Multi-Discipline Chinese Evaluation Suite for Foundation Models

**2023** | Co-Author

Developing a multi-level multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models. This work introduces C-Eval, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating Chinese language understanding capabilities of foundation models across various disciplines and difficulty levels.

### Composing Parameter-Efficient Modules with Arithmetic Operations

**2023** | Co-Author

Composing parameter-efficient modules with arithmetic operations. This work explores how to combine different parameter-efficient fine-tuning modules using arithmetic operations to create more powerful and flexible models.
